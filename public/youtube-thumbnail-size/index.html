Video image size guide

YouTube Thumbnail Size: 1280 × 720 px

A 1280 × 720 px canvas gives you a clear 16:9 thumbnail starting point. It works well for a face, product, or visual result paired with a short title that remains readable when the image is shown small.

Build a thumbnail that reads quickly

At thumbnail scale, contrast and hierarchy matter more than decoration. Start with one clear subject, reserve a simple area for text, and avoid placing important details at the extreme edges. Before exporting, zoom out in the editor to check whether the title is still legible.

SizeCraft can crop the source image to 16:9, add a shape crop or privacy cover, place multiple text layers, and add a simple dimension marker when the image needs a measurement callout.

Thumbnail checklist

  1. Choose 1280 × 720.
    The wide canvas gives the layout a familiar video-card shape.
  2. Place the subject first.
    Move and scale the image before adding text so the crop supports the story.
  3. Keep the message short.
    Use one or two text layers and give the words enough contrast.
  4. Inspect the small view.
    A design that looks good only when large may not work in a list of videos.

Common questions

Can I make a custom thumbnail ratio?

Yes. Choose a custom canvas in the editor if your project needs another output size.

Can I add several pieces of text?

Yes. Text is stored as separate layers so each item can be selected and edited independently.
