Social image size guide

Instagram Story Size: 1080 × 1920 px

A 1080 × 1920 px portrait canvas gives a 9:16 working area for Stories and other full-screen vertical posts. Start with this size when a photo, short announcement, product preview, or tutorial needs to fill a phone screen.

Open the 1080 × 1920 editorSee square post size

Design for a tall screen

Vertical layouts are narrow, so put the subject and the most important words on the central part of the canvas. Keep extra space near the top and bottom for interface overlays that may appear when a viewer opens the story. This is a practical composition suggestion, not a substitute for checking the current platform interface.

With SizeCraft you can load a photo, select the Story / Reel preset, move the image inside the frame, and add text or privacy stickers without sending the original file to a server.

Make a clean story image

  1. Start at 1080 × 1920.
    This keeps the working canvas consistent from the beginning.
  2. Check the crop at full height.
    Make sure heads, product corners, and labels remain inside the visible area.
  3. Use a small number of overlays.
    A short headline and one privacy or measurement mark are often easier to scan than a crowded design.

Common questions

Is 9:16 the only option?

No. The editor includes several social, marketplace, and website presets plus a custom canvas option.

Can I resize text after placing it?

Yes. Select the text layer in the editor to adjust its size, font, color, and position.
