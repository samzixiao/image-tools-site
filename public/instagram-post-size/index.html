Social image size guide

Instagram Post Size: 1080 × 1080 px

A square 1080 × 1080 px canvas is a practical starting point for an Instagram feed post. Use it when you want a balanced crop that works well for product photos, announcements, portraits, and simple quote graphics.

Open the 1080 × 1080 editorSee Instagram Story size

What should the canvas include?

Keep the main subject near the visual center and leave breathing room around text or product edges. The square format gives you the same width and height, so it is easy to reuse across a feed grid. If the original photo is tall or wide, crop the least important background first instead of shrinking the subject until it becomes difficult to read.

SizeCraft lets you load an image locally, choose the square preset, reposition the crop, add shapes, privacy covers, text, and dimension markers, then export the result. The editing work happens in your browser.

Quick workflow

  1. Choose the canvas.
    Open the editor and select Instagram Post / 1080 × 1080.
  2. Crop with intention.
    Move the image until faces, products, or labels are not cut off.
  3. Add only useful overlays.
    Use text, a shape crop, a privacy sticker, or a dimension marker when it helps the viewer.
  4. Export and check.
    Open the downloaded file before publishing and check small text at phone size.

Common questions

Can I use a different image ratio?

Yes. Use the editor's other presets or custom canvas. This page is specifically for a square starting point.

Will my image be uploaded?

The current editor is designed for browser-side editing. Select a file locally and export your result from the page.
