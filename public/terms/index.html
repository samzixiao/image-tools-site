Terms of Use

Use SizeCraft responsibly and review every exported image before publishing it. These short notes describe the current free browser tool and may change as the project grows.

Use the tool responsibly

You are responsible for the images you load, edit, and export. Do not use the editor to infringe copyright, impersonate another person, expose private information, or create content that violates the rules of a marketplace or platform.

Accuracy and platform requirements

Preset sizes and conversion helpers are practical editing aids, not a guarantee of approval by a social network, marketplace, printer, or other service. Requirements can change. Confirm the latest instructions for the destination where you plan to publish.

Availability

The project is provided as an evolving utility. A feature may change or be unavailable while it is being improved. If an action produces an unexpected result, please report the steps and a small non-sensitive example through the feedback page.
